Electrical Services in Santa Monica

Coastal homes in Santa Monica face their own electrical challenges — from salt-air corrosion on outdoor fixtures to older panels in the historic neighborhoods around Ocean Park and Sunset Park. AB Electrical handles them all with code-compliant, weather-rated work built to last.

Common Work in Santa Monica

Exterior fixture replacement is one of our most common Santa Monica jobs. Coastal weather is rough on lighting and outdoor outlets, and we keep a regular rotation of waterproof sconces, LED floods, and weather-rated GFCI outlets ready to install. We also do a steady volume of LED retrofits in older homes.
