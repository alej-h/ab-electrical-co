EV Charger Installation

Got a new electric vehicle? AB Electrical installs Level 2 home chargers so you can charge faster and more reliably than a standard outlet allows.

The 220V Circuit

Most Level 2 EV chargers run on either a 30A or 50A 220V circuit. A standard installation covers the run from your panel up to 15 feet, one receptacle, and a Siemens or Murray circuit breaker.

  • 220V 30A New Circuit
  • 220V 50A New Circuit

Site Visit Required for Full Quote

EV charger installs vary too much for a single flat rate — every house is different. The total cost depends on:

  • Distance from your panel to where the charger will mount
  • Whether your panel has spare capacity (or needs a sub-panel added)
  • Conduit routing — through walls, attics, garage, or underground
  • Charger model and mounting requirements
  • Permit and inspection requirements for your city
