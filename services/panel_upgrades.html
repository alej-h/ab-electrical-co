Panel Work & New Circuits

Adding a new appliance, EV charger, hot tub, or workshop circuit? AB Electrical runs new circuits straight from your panel — including a standard breaker and a clean install up to 15 feet from the panel. For full panel replacements, we'll come out, look at your existing service, and quote it on-site.

New Circuit Work

Each new circuit installation includes labor for runs up to 15 feet from the panel, one receptacle, and a standard Siemens or Murray circuit breaker. Additional distance or extra outlets may require an on-site assessment.

  • 120V 20A New Circuit
  • 220V 30A New Circuit
  • 220V 50A New Circuit

Sump Pump Connections

  • 120V Sump Pump Connection (up to 20' from existing power)
  • 220V Sump Pump Connection (up to 20' from existing power)

Material costs additional if needed.

Bell Boxes & Junction Work
