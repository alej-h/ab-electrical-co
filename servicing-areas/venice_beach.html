Electrical Services in Venice Beach

From classic Venice bungalows to modern walk-streets and live/work spaces, AB Electrical has worked on just about every type of property in the neighborhood. We understand the local permitting process and can get your project moving without surprises.

Common Work in Venice Beach

Venice's mix of original bungalows, additions, and converted live/work spaces means electrical work here often involves adding circuits, upgrading outlets to GFCI in kitchens and bathrooms, and modernizing lighting. We pull permits where required and keep installs clean and inspector-ready.
