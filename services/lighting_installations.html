Lighting Installation

Good lighting transforms a space — and modern LED fixtures save real money on energy bills compared to older incandescent or fluorescent options. AB Electrical handles lighting jobs of every size, from a single fixture swap to whole-home recessed lighting and full exterior overhauls. Every install is tested for proper switch and circuit operation before we leave.
