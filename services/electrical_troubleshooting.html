Electrical Troubleshooting

Flickering lights, breakers that won't reset, dead outlets, mystery buzzing — electrical issues are stressful because you can't see them. AB Electrical diagnoses the problem and fixes it on the spot whenever possible. Troubleshooting is built into most of our standard repair prices, so you're not paying twice.

Common Issues We Fix

  • Tripping breakers and short-circuit diagnosis
  • Dead outlets and partial-power issues
  • Flickering, dimming, or buzzing lights
  • Burning smells or scorched outlets (turn off the breaker and call us right away)
  • GFCI / AFCI failures and nuisance trips
  • Switches that no longer control fixtures
  • Whole-home electrical inspections

Diagnostic + Replacement

Most of our standard outlet, switch, and dimmer prices already include troubleshooting the existing connection, replacing the device if it's faulty, and re-splicing the junction box. Common examples:

  • Decora Duplex 15A outlet (troubleshoot + replace if needed)
  • GFCI Duplex 20A outlet (troubleshoot + replace if needed)
  • 30A / 40A / 50A 3-wire receptacles
  • Decora Single-Pole switch
  • Decora 3-Way switch
  • Decora 4-Way switch
  • Diva LED Dimmer (150W)
  • Smart Dimmer

How We Diagnose

We use proper diagnostic tools to find the root cause — not just the symptom. You'll get a clear explanation of what's wrong, what we recommend, and what it'll cost before any work begins.
